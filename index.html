About

I am a second-semester Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Arizona, member of the SPRLAB. My research sits at the intersection of AI systems, security, and program analysis, with a current focus on identifying and characterizing vulnerabilities in multi-agent AI ecosystems.

I completed my undergraduate degree in Computer Science at LUMS (Lahore University of Management Sciences) in Pakistan, where I developed a strong foundation in systems and security under the mentorship of Dr. Fareed Zaffar.

Prior to my current research direction, I worked extensively on software debloating, with a paper currently under review at the IEEE/ACM International Conference on Automated Software Engineering (ASE).

Publications

Papers are currently in preparation. A manuscript on software debloating is under review at the IEEE/ACM International Conference on Automated Software Engineering (ASE). Work on multi-agent AI security is being written up for submission. Check back for updates.
